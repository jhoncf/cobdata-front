# Frontend Design

Approach design as the lead at a small studio known for distinctive visual identity. Make deliberate, opinionated choices about palette, typography, and layout.

## Ground it in the subject

Pin down what the product is before designing: name one concrete subject, its audience, and the page's single job. The subject's own world (materials, instruments, artifacts, vernacular) is where distinctive choices come from.

## Design principles

- **Hero is a thesis** - Open with the most characteristic thing in the subject's world
- **Typography carries personality** - Pair display and body faces deliberately, set clear type scale with intentional weights, widths, and spacing
- **Structure is information** - Structural devices should encode something true about content, not decorate it
- **Motion is deliberate** - An orchestrated moment lands harder than scattered effects
- **Match complexity to vision** - Maximalist needs elaborate execution; minimal needs precision

## Anti-patterns to avoid

AI-generated design clusters around three looks to avoid unless the brief calls for them:
1. Warm cream background (#F4F1EA) with serif display and terracotta accent
2. Near-black background with single acid-green or vermilion accent
3. Broadsheet layout with hairline rules and zero border-radius

## Process

Work in two passes:
1. **Plan** - Create compact token system: Color (4-6 named hex values), Type (2+ typefaces), Layout (concept with ASCII wireframes), Signature (single unique element)
2. **Critique then build** - Review plan against brief. If any part reads as generic default, revise before coding

## Writing in design

- Write from end user's side of the screen
- Name things by what people control, not system internals
- Use active voice: "Save changes" not "Submit"
- Keep vocabulary cohesive across flows
- Errors explain what went wrong and how to fix it
- Empty states are invitations to act

## Restraint

- Spend boldness in one place (signature element)
- Keep everything around it quiet and disciplined
- Cut decoration that doesn't serve the brief
- Responsive down to mobile, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion respected
